# HivePulse features

The full list of what HivePulse does today, where to find it, and which document describes it.
The same list is shown to users at `/release-notes` on the website (source: `lib/releaseNotes.ts`), and
the apps open that page from **Settings → Release notes**. Keep the three in step: when a feature ships,
add it to `lib/releaseNotes.ts` (a line in `RELEASES`, and in `FEATURES` if it is new), write or extend its
help page in `app/[locale]/help/[slug]/content/`, add a News entry in `lib/news.ts`, and update this file.

Platforms: **W** website, **I** iPhone app, **A** Android app.

## The beekeeping year

| Feature | Where | W | I | A | Contract |
|---------|-------|:-:|:-:|:-:|----------|
| Endless timeline of what to do when (feeding, inspections, swarm control, drone frames, moving and extracting per honey, varroa, winter), in five languages | Dashboard menu; calendar icon in the apiary list toolbar | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Beekeeping Year |
| Moved to the beekeeper's place by country and postal code (about 4 days per degree of latitude), adjustable by hand | Profile; Settings → Region | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Beekeeping Year |
| The same year as one picture: twelve months across, a row per subject, a bar per job, overlapping jobs stacked into lanes, a photograph of the thing itself; a forage is one bar from the move to the end of its harvest | Beekeeping year → Overview | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Beekeeping Year |
| On a phone the website replaces the picture with the year running down the screen, opened at today; the apps draw it wider than the screen and drag it sideways instead | Beekeeping year → Overview | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Beekeeping Year |
| A photograph of the job -- the plant in flower, a capped queen cell, the mite on a bee -- in both views, with its author and licence | Beekeeping year | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | — |

The content is `backend/app/beekeeping_year.py` (dates for central Germany), the arithmetic `backend/app/season.py`.
The ribbon's arithmetic is `lib/yearRibbon.ts`, ported to `YearRibbon.kt` and `YearRibbon.swift`; the three
test suites mirror each other case for case, because the same year has to come out the same shape on all
three. The photographs and their credits are `lib/yearPhotos.ts`, `YearPhotos.kt` and `YearPhotos.swift`,
all four files generated by `scripts/fetch_year_photos.py` and listed in `docs/photo-credits.md` -- most
are CC BY or CC BY-SA, so a credit that drifted between clients would be a licence breach. The apps carry
the pictures inside the app (1.2 MB), because at the apiary there is usually no signal. Five jobs still
have no usable photograph: issue 412.
Guide values only; medicines subject to the approvals of the country.

## Statistics, data and community

Per-hive charts, overview of all apiaries, community statistics, JSON/CSV export; the hornet tracker with
reports, community photos, map and named traps. Supporters get a regional health map (varroa, mood, swarm, brood)
in the Members tab, drawn as soft round patches that blend into each other — the same on web, Android and iPhone.
See the help pages `hive-stats`, `community-stats`,
`data-export`, `hornet-tracker`, `hornet-traps`.

## Account

Sign in with e-mail, Apple or Google; English, German, French, Spanish and Polish; change password (not
for accounts made through Apple or Google); delete the account and all data (also revokes Apple access);
crash reports to an EU-region Sentry; a guided tour.

**Send feedback** sits in Settings under Help on all three clients. On Android it opens the Play listing,
because feedback sent from there is what reaches the Play Console's testing feedback page; on the iPhone
and the website it opens a mail with the version and build already in the subject.

## Navigation (the same on iPhone and Android)

Tabs: Apiaries, Scan, Hornets, Members, Settings. Apiary list toolbar: statistics, QR batches, map of
moves, beekeeping year. Every create action is the amber labelled button at the bottom right.
Settings: custom fields, profile, change password, reminders, region, export, admin, help and tour, release
notes, diagnostics, log out, danger zone. See `CLAUDE.md`.

## Wording

German: Bienenstand, Volk, Kontrolle, QR-Batch (Beutentyp for the type of hive). French: rucher, ruche,
visite. Spanish: colmenar, colmena, revisión. Use these in new strings on every surface.
